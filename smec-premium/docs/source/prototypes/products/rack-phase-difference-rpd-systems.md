## What is Rack Phase Difference (RPD) Systems? _(hidden in prototype)_

Rack Phase Difference (RPD) monitoring supports awareness of relative rack positions in jack-up jacking arrangements. Engineering starts with the actual leg, rack and pinion configuration, measurement method and OEM-defined operating limits.


## How it works

**Measurement principle**

RPD monitors relative rack displacement/phase within jack-up jacking arrangements, using position measurement and rig-specific geometry.

**Display and alarms**

Differences can be displayed, trended and alarmed; allowable thresholds are established by the jack-up OEM/design authority.

**Integration**

RPD data may interface with the jacking controller, but its protective authority must be explicitly defined.

**Sensor assurance**

Sensor mounting, calibration, resolution and signal-loss detection determine the credibility of the displayed difference.

## FAQs

**Is RPD a standalone jacking controller?**

It is primarily a monitoring/control-interface function and should be engineered with the jacking system.

**Can existing position sensors be reused?**

Possibly, after checking accuracy, calibration and availability.

**What drawings are essential?**

Jacking arrangement, sensor layouts, existing PLC architecture and OEM operating limits.
