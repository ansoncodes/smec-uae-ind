## How it works

**Emergency announcements**

PAGA distributes prioritised paging, tones and general alarms across defined facility zones.

**Talkback**

Driller/talkback provides operational two-way communication between rig locations; it is not interchangeable with emergency PAGA.

**Coverage**

Speaker layout, noise mapping and intelligibility testing determine audibility in operating areas.

**Resilience**

Power backup, network redundancy, hazardous-area stations and F&G interfaces follow the facility emergency plan.

## FAQs

**Is driller talkback the same as PAGA?**

No. Talkback supports operational two-way communications; PAGA distributes announcements and alarms.

**Can PAGA interface with F&G?**

Yes, with a documented cause-and-effect and alarm priority philosophy.

**What determines speaker quantity?**

Noise conditions, area layout, intelligibility requirements and site standards.
