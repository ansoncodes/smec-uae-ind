## How it works

**Modular infrastructure**

The E-House integrates switchgear, transformers, MCCs, automation and auxiliaries within a prefabricated structure engineered for transportation and installation.

**Structure and logistics**

Lifting points, floor loading, transport envelope, cable entry, wind/seismic design and foundation interfaces are developed with the electrical layout.

**Environmental protection**

Heat-load calculations, air filtration, cooling redundancy, corrosion exposure and ingress protection shape the building envelope.

**Factory integration**

A complete integrated FAT can check electrical, controls, alarms and auxiliaries before shipment, reducing on-site interface risk.

## FAQs

**Is an E-House the same as a container?**

No. An E-House is an engineered electrical facility with defined structural, electrical and environmental requirements.

**Can an E-House contain both switchgear and automation?**

Yes, provided separation, access, heat load and applicable safety requirements are addressed.

**What is needed to size an E-House?**

Equipment list, SLD, site conditions, transport restrictions and service-access requirements.
