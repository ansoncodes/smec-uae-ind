## How it works

**Safety functions**

Each trip is defined from initiating sensor through logic solver to final element and required safe state.

**Logic solver**

Safety PLC, I/O, redundancy and segregation are chosen against the safety requirements specification and project functional safety assessment.

**Operations**

Bypasses, resets, trip diagnostics and maintenance access require controlled procedures.

**Validation**

Cause-and-effect FAT, loop checks and final-element proof testing verify implemented functions; SIL cannot be inferred from PLC certification alone.

## FAQs

**Can ESD be integrated with plant SCADA?**

Status and approved commands can be interfaced while maintaining required safety independence.

**Is every ESD system SIL-rated?**

Not necessarily; the required integrity follows the project hazard/risk assessment.

**What documentation is essential?**

SRS, cause-and-effect, shutdown narratives, I/O list and applicable site standards.
