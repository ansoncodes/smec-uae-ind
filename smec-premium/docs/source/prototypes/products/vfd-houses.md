## How it works

**Drive selection**

The motor torque-speed curve, overload duration, operating speed range and supply characteristics determine whether LV or MV VFD architecture is appropriate.

**Power quality**

Input harmonics, generator interaction, DC-link energy and switching transients are addressed through appropriate transformer, filtering and drive topology.

**Motor interface**

Output filters, insulation stress, motor cable length, grounding and common-mode currents are assessed to avoid premature motor or bearing problems.

**Thermal management**

Drive losses, ambient temperature, ventilation paths and cooling redundancy determine enclosure/HVAC design.

## FAQs

**When should an SCR rig consider a VFD conversion?**

When lifecycle cost, motor replacement, controls, spare parts and power quality justify a system-level upgrade.

**Can the VFD house be supplied as a packaged unit?**

Yes, subject to transport, site access, cooling and project specification.

**What determines VFD size?**

Motor data, duty cycle, overload requirement, ambient conditions and driven-machine torque.
