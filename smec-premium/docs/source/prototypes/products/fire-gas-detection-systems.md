## How it works

**Field devices**

Combustible/toxic gas, flame, smoke and heat detection technologies are selected against credible hazards and coverage requirements.

**Logic and response**

F&G panels implement annunciation, voting and cause-and-effect outputs to ESD, PAGA or other defined interfaces.

**Coverage**

Detector placement requires hazard/dispersion and visibility considerations; quantity alone does not establish adequate coverage.

**Lifecycle**

Calibration, bump/proof testing, device diagnostics and records are part of maintaining detection performance.

## FAQs

**Does F&G automatically include ESD?**

The systems may interface, but scope and independent safety functions must be defined.

**Can old detectors be retained?**

Only after suitability, certification, lifecycle and calibration checks.

**What inputs are needed?**

Plot plan, hazard study, detector schedule, cause-and-effect and site standards.
