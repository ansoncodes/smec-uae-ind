## FAQs

**Is Gas Watch the same as a general refinery gas detector?**

No; drilling mud-gas applications and fixed-area gas detection have different measurement arrangements.

**Can alarms be integrated into DMS or rig PLC?**

Yes, subject to approved interfaces and alarm ownership.

**What details are required for an RFQ?**

Gas type, range, number of points, hazardous-area classification and required outputs.
