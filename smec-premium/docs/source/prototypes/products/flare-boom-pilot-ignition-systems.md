## How it works

**Ignition path**

An ignition control panel energises pilot ignition devices through specified cables, field junction boxes and ignition hardware.

**Pilot arrangement**

Pilot count, tip arrangement, distance and environmental exposure influence the ignition architecture.

**Feedback**

Flame proving or ignition status indication is specified separately from the act of generating a spark.

**Field installation**

HV insulation, earthing, Ex-rated equipment and safe test procedures are essential for offshore and process-area installations.

## Upgrades & retrofits

- Replace ignition cards, HV cabling and field junction boxes as needed.
- Add pilot points or local/remote initiation stations to an approved sequence.
- Introduce flame status feedback where supported by the pilot arrangement.
Retrofit scope depends on existing OEM interfaces, component condition, available space, shutdown windows and the approval basis of any safety-related functions.

## FAQs

**Can an old ignition panel be replaced while retaining pilot hardware?**

Sometimes, after compatibility and condition checks.

**Is flame detection included?**

It can be specified as an interface or package element, but must be explicitly defined.

**What should the RFQ include?**

Pilot count, existing ignition arrangement, drawings, hazardous-area rating and site conditions.
