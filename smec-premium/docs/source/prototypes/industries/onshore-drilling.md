# Onshore Drilling

**Reliable power and control for land drilling operations.**

Integrated electrical and automation solutions for new-build, upgrade and operational support of onshore rigs.

### Rig Power

Power generation and motor-drive systems

- SCR/VFD controls for drilling loads
- Generator synchronisation and load management
- Switchgear and motor-control panels

### Drilling Controls

Operational visibility and automation

- Drill monitoring and HMI upgrades
- PLC/control-system modernisation
- Field instrumentation and alarm integration

### Rig Upgrades

Brownfield work and support

- Legacy equipment assessment
- Control-panel replacement and rewiring
- Testing and commissioning
