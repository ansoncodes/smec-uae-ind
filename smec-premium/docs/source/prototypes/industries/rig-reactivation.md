# Rig Reactivation

**Bring critical rig systems back into service with confidence.**

Targeted assessment, obsolescence management and upgrade of electrical, control and safety systems during rig reactivation.

### Assess & Recover

Establish the condition of dormant equipment

- Electrical inspection and system inventory
- Drive, switchgear and control diagnostics
- Obsolescence and spare-parts review

### Modernise

Restore critical operating functions

- SCR/VFD and PLC/HMI upgrades
- PMS and protection-system review
- Instrumentation and alarm restoration

### Test & Return

Prepare for operational readiness

- Functional checks and interlock verification
- FAT/SAT planning
- Commissioning and documentation
