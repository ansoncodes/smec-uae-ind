# Engineering Glossary

A quick reference for common electrical, automation, oil and gas terms.

## Authored reference content (in prototype)

### ACB

Air Circuit Breaker — low-voltage switching and protection device used in power distribution.

### BOP

Blowout Preventer — well-control equipment used to isolate or control wellbore pressure.

### CMMS

Computerised Maintenance Management System — manages maintenance activities, work orders and asset records.

### DCS

Distributed Control System — process-control architecture with distributed controllers and operator interfaces.

### E-House

Prefabricated electrical building housing switchgear, drives, control panels and associated services.

### ESD

Emergency Shutdown — system designed to move equipment or a process to a defined safe state.

### FAT

Factory Acceptance Test — documented functional verification before dispatch.

### F&G

Fire and Gas — detection and associated alarm or protective functions.

### HMI

Human–Machine Interface — operator display and interaction with control equipment.

### I/O

Input/Output — field signals exchanged with a control system.

### MCC

Motor Control Centre — electrical assembly for controlling and protecting motors.

### PAGA

Public Address and General Alarm — facility-wide announcements and alarm notification.

### PCC

Power Control Centre — electrical distribution and switching assembly.

### PLC

Programmable Logic Controller — industrial controller for automation logic.

### PMS

Power Management System — generator and electrical-load coordination.

### RPD

Rack Phase Difference — monitoring of relative rack/pinion displacement in jack-up jacking systems.

### RTU

Remote Terminal Unit — field telemetry and control device.

### SAT

Site Acceptance Test — system verification after installation.

### SCADA

Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition — monitoring and supervisory control platform.

### SCR

Silicon-Controlled Rectifier — thyristor-based power conversion technology.

### SIL

Safety Integrity Level — risk-reduction performance classification for a safety instrumented function.

### VFD

Variable Frequency Drive — controls AC motor speed by varying output frequency and voltage.
