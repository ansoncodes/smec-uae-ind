# Engineering Checklists

Practical preparation lists for upgrades, inspections and RFQs.

## Authored reference content (in prototype)

### Rig Reactivation Electrical Readiness

['Confirm equipment inventory and drawings', 'Inspect generator, switchgear and drive condition', 'Review PLC/HMI program backups', 'Check alarms, trips and protective interlocks', 'Verify instrument calibration status', 'Plan functional tests and commissioning records']

### PLC / SCADA Retrofit Planning

['Record existing PLC and I/O inventory', 'Capture field signal and protocol requirements', 'Confirm shutdown and changeover window', 'Define alarm, historian and HMI requirements', 'Prepare migration and rollback plan', 'Agree FAT/SAT test cases']

### Vessel Drydock Electrical Review

['Review UPS batteries and autonomy', 'Check VFD fans, capacitors and fault logs', 'Back up PLC/HMI/drive parameters', 'Test generator synchronisation and load sharing', 'Inspect emergency power and distribution', 'Plan cable insulation and protection testing']

### Control Panel RFQ Checklist

['Supply single-line diagrams and load list', 'Provide I/O list and control philosophy', 'Specify voltage, frequency and fault levels', 'State ambient conditions and enclosure location', 'Confirm standards and inspection requirements', 'Identify delivery schedule and installation scope']

### Pipeline SCADA Project Inputs

['List stations, RTUs and telemetry points', 'Define protocols and communications links', 'Specify event logging and alarm priorities', 'Review cybersecurity and remote access', 'Confirm local control and fail-safe behavior', 'Agree FAT/SAT and site integration scope']
