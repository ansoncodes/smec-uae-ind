All electrical and automation systems — MV/LV Switchgear, MCC rooms, VFD panels, PLC/SCADA, relay panels, HVAC, Fire & Gas systems — are integrated, wired, and FAT-tested in our Indian manufacturing facility before shipment.

This eliminates:

- On-site troubleshooting
- Rework costs
- Integration failures
- Project delays

This fully integrated build ensures every subsystem communicates seamlessly the moment it reaches site, eliminating interface mismatches between electrical and automation packages.
It also standardizes quality across all units — a major advantage for EPCs managing multi-location or fast-track project rollouts.

## Technical Scope: What's Inside an E-House?

### A modular E-House typically contains:

**Power Systems**

- MV Switchgear (5kV–33kV)
- LV Switchgear & MCCs
- VFD Panels
- Power factor control
- Relay & metering systems

**Automation & Control**

- PLC/DCS Panels
- SCADA Integration
- RTUs, marshalling, & network rooms
- Alarm monitoring systems

**Safety & Environmental Systems**

- Integrated Fire & Gas Detection
- HVAC (industrial & explosion-proof)
- Ex-proof & non-Ex-rated enclosures

**Structural & Utility**

- Prefabricated steel structure
- Cable routing systems
- Lighting & earthing systems

### Ideal Applications

- Oil & Gas Pipelines and Pumping Stations
- Refinery & Petrochemical Processing Units
- Power Generation & Substation Projects
- Mining and Metals Processing Plants
- Offshore Platforms and Marine Terminals
- Large Infrastructure & EPC Projects
